# Little Chat

## Register
product

## Users
One Instagram creator replacing their own ManyChat comment-to-message automations before their free allowance runs out. This is a private tool for its owner, not a public SaaS product.

## Product Purpose
The owner signs in at an unlinked /admin URL, pastes a reel link, chooses keywords and writes replies. The server handles matching Instagram notifications and records delivery outcomes. Success means the basic workflow works with minimal maintenance and no unnecessary screens.

## Brand Personality
Simple, direct, practical. The owner explicitly requested an extremely simple interface and prioritizes working functionality over decoration.

## Anti-references
Avoid marketing pages, upsells, visual workflow builders, decorative dashboards, and unnecessary setup interviews.

## Design Principles
Use ordinary forms and explicit status text. Keep account credentials private. Never imply an automation is live before Instagram is connected. Make delivery failures visible and fixable. Public pages never link to admin.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Use accessible defaults: labeled native controls, keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts and no decorative motion. No additional accommodations were specified.
